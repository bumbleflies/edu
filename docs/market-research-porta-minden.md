# Venue & audience research — bumble:futurespace in Minden / Porta Westfalica

Working notes for deciding a room and an outreach plan for the kids' robotics
courses (`Robot Explorer` €35/month, 8 weeks, `Code the Machine` €49/month,
10 weeks, ages 8–14, small groups, one mbot2 per child), plus the
"zwischen den Jahren" holiday camp (€99, 28–30 December 2026, ages 8–12).

Researched October 2026. Prices marked **[confirmed]** come from a published
fee schedule; **[listing]** = an active property ad (moves fast);
**[on request]** = not published.

---

## 0. The constraint that decides everything: what the room budget really is

Billing is now **per month** — €35 (Robot Explorer) and €49 (Code the Machine) —
which moves the room budget by roughly a factor of three. Per cohort of 8
children, counting a term as 2 months (8 weeks) and 2,5 months (10 weeks):

| | Robot Explorer | Code the Machine |
|---|---|---|
| Fee per child | €35 / month | €49 / month |
| Revenue per child per term | €70 | €122,50 |
| Cohort revenue (8 kids) | €560 | €980 |
| Contact hours (8×1,25 h / 10×1,5 h) | 10 h | 15 h |
| **Revenue per room-hour** | **€56** | **€65** |

A room rented by the hour can now carry **€11–13/h** at 20 % of revenue. The
cheapest officially available classroom in Minden (€17/h) lands at **26–30 %**
instead of the 85 % it used to eat, and the €12/h Differentiation room in the
Haus der Bildung sits at **19–21 %** — right on the guideline.

**Enrolments needed to keep a fixed monthly room at ≤20 % of revenue**
(average €42/child/month, the midpoint of €35 and €49):

| Monthly room cost | Enrolments needed |
|---|---|
| €200/month | ~24 children |
| €400/month | ~48 children |
| €600/month | ~71 children |
| €17/h pay-per-hour venue | viable for one to two cohorts (~€170–255 per term) |

**The catch:** a two-month term billed monthly collects two months of fees, not
twelve. Between terms there is no revenue at all, so a fixed monthly room is
only covered by *year-round* enrolment — back-to-back terms — or by the
higher-margin formats below. Budget the room against twelve months of rent
while the course calendar still fills fewer.

### What this means practically

1. **An hourly room is affordable now, but still the worst deal.** One cohort
   at €17/h costs ~€170–255 per term against €560–980 of revenue. The trap is
   that every extra cohort in the same rented hour pays the rent again, while a
   fixed monthly room gets cheaper the more you fill it.
2. **Either get space for free/peppercorn** (Verein, Kirchengemeinde, school,
   partner, youth facility) **or take a fixed monthly room and fill it hard**
   (multiple cohorts per evening, bigger groups).
3. **Fix the price mix.** The weekly course is the cheap funnel. The room gets
   paid for by higher-margin formats:
   - birthday "Roboter-Party", 2 h, €120–150 → €60–75 per room-hour
   - holiday camp, 3 days × 4 h, €99/child, 12 kids → €1 188 per 12 contact
     hours, i.e. **€99/room-hour** (~1,5–1,8× the weekly course)
   - school AG / OGS contracts, company-funded employee-child programmes
   Each of those is worth more per room-hour than the weekly course.

Room wish-list for mbot2 groups: **45–70 m²**, tables (not rows of chairs),
**8+ usable sockets** (or your own power strips), decent WiFi, storage for
robots between sessions, WC, and parent parking/loading near the door.

## 2. Who exactly you are selling to

**Primary:** parents aged ~30–50 in Minden, Porta Westfalica, Bad Oeynhausen,
Hille and Petershagen with a child aged 8–14. They are choosing between you and
football/handball training, music school, and *free* holiday programmes. The
three things that make them book: **a real robot** (not a screen), **small
groups**, and **a fixed weekly slot that fits around school**.

**Secondary:** teachers and school administrators (AG/OGS offers), companies
recruiting MINT staff (employee-child holiday programmes), grandparents
(paying for birthday gifts), and libraries/museums looking for MINT content.

**Market size:** Kreis Minden-Lübbecke ~315 000 residents; Minden ~82 000,
Porta Westfalica ~36 000. Radio Westfalica alone reaches **40,9 % of the
district's population daily** — the reach is there; the challenge is
conversion, not audience size.

### Competitors you must position against

| Competitor | What they do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **codefryx** (Minden, Löhne, Espelkamp) | Weekly programming courses Scratch→Python, robotics, 3D printing; holiday camps; school programmes; parent workshops; trial courses; strong reels on Instagram; part of Code Week Minden-Lübbecke | Closest thing to a direct rival. They win on Instagram/short video. Differentiate on: real mbot2 hardware, smaller groups, showcase event, Porta Westfalica presence. |
| **zdi "MINT for all"** (2026–2029) | Free/low-cost MINT for ages 8–14 across the whole Kreis: MINT-Print, holiday workshops, camps, MINT-Fest, MINTmach Club | Free competitor for your exact target group — and your single best distribution partner. Join the network rather than fight it. |
| **Kulturrucksack NRW / Kulturbüro Minden** | Free cultural projects for ages 10–14 in Minden | Same age bracket, zero price. Also a potential funding application for your format. |
| Stadtbibliothek, Mindener Museum, Schulsternwarte | Free MINT/culture workshops | Lower commitment than a 8–10 week course; use them as taster feeders. |
| Minden youth houses, KUBU, Anne Frank | Free open children's/youth programmes | Compete for the same afternoons. |

## 3. Ways to reach the audience

### A. Partnerships and institutions (highest conversion, lowest cost)

1. **zdi Minden-Lübbecke / MINT for all** — become a listed partner
   (0571 807-13560). Their network includes der.Hive, Codefryx, Schulsternwarte,
   HSBI and the MINTlabs — one conversation buys you credibility plus access to
   a lot of parent-facing channels.
2. **Code Week Minden-Lübbecke**, with the Code Week running **10–25 October
   2026** (i.e. right now). Regional hub pages list partner events; a large
   share of Code Week offers are free and need no registration. Run a free
   90-minute taster and get listed on `codeweek.de`.
3. **MINT-Fest / MINT-Fest im Schlossgarten (Minden)** — take an Erlebnisstation
   (a robot following a line on the floor). Highest-visibility single event in
   the region for exactly your product.
4. **Schools** — Minden: all Grundschulen plus weiterführende Schulen
   (Herder-Gymnasium, Ratsgymnasium, Besselgymnasium, Kurt-Tucholsky-
   Gesamtschule, Domschule, Mosaik-Schule, Eine-Welt-Schule, PRIMUS-Schule);
   Porta: Gymnasium Porta Westfalica, Realschule and Gesamtschule.
   Target the head teacher, the MINT coordinator, and the **Schulpflegschaft /
   Elternbeirat** (parents who already send mail to every family).
   - **Legal note:** the NRW OGS framework excludes *commercial* providers and
     for-profit tutoring institutes from OGS partnerships (§ 55 SchulG), while
     non-profit partners may get school rooms **free of charge** (Nr. 6.2,
     Runderlass of 02.07.2024). So: as a business you sell via AGs, parent
     letters, and holiday offers; the free-room OGS route needs a non-profit
     entity (or a non-profit partner) in front.
5. **Familiengrundschulzentren (FGZ)** at Mosaik-Schule and Eine-Welt-Schule
   Minden — each received ~€10 000 for parent-facing offers. Pitch a
   parent+child robot session into the **Elterncafé**; the FGZ does the
   inviting.
6. **OGS providers in Minden** — VHS Minden/Bad Oeynhausen runs OGS in the
   city; Elsa-Brandström-Jugendhilfe Minden runs many school sites. They need
   afternoon programme content every week.
7. **Stadtbibliothek Minden** (free 60–120 min workshops as an
   *außerschulischer Lernort*) and **Stadtbücherei Porta Westfalica**
   (Kirchhofsweg 6). A "Bücherei-Roboter-Nachmittag" reaches exactly the

8. **Familienzentren / Kitas** (e.g. Familienzentrum Kunterbunt, Eisbergen) and
   **Kirchengemeinden** — bulletin boards, parent café slots, and in-village
   trust.
9. **Sports clubs** (TuS Holzhausen/Porta, SV 1860 Minden, Union Minden) —
   noticeboards and a shared-parent audience; consider a joint holiday offer.
